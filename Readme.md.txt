# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** built using **Tableau**. The dashboard analyzes product, seller, pricing, discount, rating, and customer review data to provide actionable business insights.

## Objectives

* Analyze product performance across different categories.
* Evaluate seller performance based on customer reviews and ratings.
* Study the relationship between price, discounts, and customer ratings.
* Understand customer engagement through reviews and rating patterns.
* Build interactive dashboards to support business decision-making.

## Tools Used

* Tableau
* SQL
* Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning)

## Dataset

The dataset contains e-commerce product information, including:

* Product Name
* Category
* Seller Name
* Price
* Discount Percentage
* Rating
* Ratings Count

## Dashboards

### 1. Executive Overview

* Total Products
* Total Categories
* Total Sellers
* Average Price
* Average Rating
* Average Discount
* Total Reviews
* Products by Category
* Average Price by Category
* Average Rating by Category
* Customer Reviews by Category

### 2. Seller Performance Analysis

* Top Reviews by Sellers
* Top Sellers by Category
* Average Rating by Sellers

### 3. Product Performance Analysis

* Top Products by Reviews
* Product Distribution by Category
* Average Rating by Category
* Price vs Rating Analysis
* Discount vs Rating Analysis

### 4. Customer Insights

* Rating Distribution
* Price vs Rating
* Discount vs Rating
* Reviews vs Rating

## Key Business Insights

* Product categories differ significantly in customer engagement.
* Seller performance varies based on customer reviews and ratings.
* Customer ratings provide valuable insights into product quality.
* Price and discount strategies can influence customer perception.
* Review volume helps identify popular products.
